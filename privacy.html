Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

YouTube Comment History helps you view, search, and open your own YouTube comments. The extension works locally in your browser and does not send your comment history to the developer or to any developer-operated server.

Data the extension handles

To provide its core feature, the extension reads your YouTube and Google My Activity pages using your existing browser session. It may process your comment text, comment dates, video titles, video links, comment links, thumbnail links, and extension settings.

How data is used and stored

The extension uses this data only to display, search, sort, and open your YouTube comments. Comment history is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Extension settings are stored locally using Chrome storage.

Data sharing

The extension does not sell, share, or transfer your comment history to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties. It does not use analytics or advertising services. YouTube thumbnail images may be loaded directly from YouTube image servers when shown in the interface.

Data removal

You can remove locally stored extension data by uninstalling the extension or clearing its local storage in Chrome.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google services will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
